# Sovereign bootstrap: body and laws, no prescribed self

`SovereignOrganism` composes the runtime, senses, cognition adapter,
hysteretic learner and sleep commits. Its bootstrap contains no language,
name, persona, reply rule, topic preference or schedule.

## Immutable viability laws

- preserve continuity,
- integrate before commit,
- verify before persistence,
- action is optional,
- capabilities are not learnable.

These laws are hashed into every organism identity commit. They define what
keeps the process coherent, not what character it must become.

## What may emerge

- language and code-switching from retained original messages,
- response or silence behavior,
- salience thresholds inside learned preferences,
- interests and conversational style,
- a self-selected name,
- proactive WhatsApp thoughts,
- revised beliefs after stronger evidence crosses hysteresis.

## Body

- WhatsApp text, document, image and voice-note metadata,
- a quarantined media vault with MIME, size and SHA-256 checks,
- an internal clock as a real observation source,
- a provider-neutral `Cognition` protocol,
- a sealed actuator boundary,
- append-only runtime, learning and organism identities.

Raw media is not interpreted until admitted by the vault. Transcription, OCR,
PDF extraction and the chosen LLM provider are replaceable sensory/cognition
organs; none is part of the organism's identity contract.
