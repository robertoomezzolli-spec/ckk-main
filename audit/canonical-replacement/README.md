# CKK / Fächer seed: canonical replacement hard gate

## Purpose

This seed does **not** add collapse, pruning, a Born sampler, Landauer heat, or a thermal bath to the native grammar.

It asks one hard question:

> Can the existing native Fächer primitives derive an effective many-to-one transition that turns unresolved alternatives into one persistent distinguishable record?

If not, the correct result is **MISSING_OPERATOR**.

## Frozen controls

- The local partner-memory process with a uniform random-pair scheduler has the exact combinatorial blocking rate
  `p_block(N)=1/(2N-3)`. This is an algorithmic control, not a natural constant.
- `H = J P_swap` does not provide outcome selection. The singlet projector commutes with the Hamiltonian, so singlet weight is conserved.
- Turning the interaction off can freeze existing entanglement; it does not purify the reduced state.
- Landauer constrains the thermodynamic accounting of logically irreversible erasure/reset. It is not an outcome-selection rule.

## Required derivation

A successful native derivation must provide all of the following without importing the desired result:

1. A complete autonomous state, including any controller or history variable that can influence the transition.
2. A native transition rule that is many-to-one at the effective record level.
3. A certificate that the resulting record is persistent under the declared future dynamics.
4. A clear separation of **record formation** from **outcome selection**.
5. A global information-flow ledger: exported, retained, or erased information must be explicit.
6. A parameter-free observable or distribution frozen before external comparison.

## Fail-closed rules

The following do not count as closure:

- partner blocking interpreted as collapse;
- decoherence or diagonal reduced density matrices interpreted as a unique outcome;
- projective measurement or Born sampling inserted as the missing operator;
- Landauer heat used to choose an outcome;
- omitted environment/controller/history variables that make an apparent many-to-one map reversible again;
- a thermal bath, temperature, Hamiltonian, metric, spacetime dimension, gauge group, CODATA number, or target natural constant inserted as a seed;
- external randomness whose physical status is not derived;
- post-hoc numerical matching.

## Scientific success criterion

A frozen native kernel generates a new parameter-free observable or distribution. Its output is sealed before external lookup or measurement and can subsequently be tested.

Until that exists, the claim **"reality is generated by canonical replacement"** remains a research hypothesis, not a derived result.
